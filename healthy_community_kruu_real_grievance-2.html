About Public Health Care

Public health care protects and improves the health of people in a community. It includes disease prevention, sanitation, safe water, health awareness and access to government services.

Major Community Health Concerns

Diabetes

Health awareness, healthy habits and regular medical care can help people manage diabetes.

Anaemia

Awareness about nutritious food and appropriate medical care is important for preventing and treating anaemia.

Dengue

Removing stagnant water helps reduce mosquito breeding places.

Poor Sanitation

Clean surroundings and proper waste disposal help protect community health.

What Our Community Can Do

  • Keep public places clean.
  • Prevent stagnant water around homes and public areas.
  • Use safe drinking water.
  • Spread awareness about hygiene and healthy habits.
  • Report civic and public-health problems to the appropriate government authority.
